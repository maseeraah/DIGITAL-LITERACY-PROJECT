GENRAL ONLINE SAFETY 

-Use strong, unique passwords for each account. Combine letters, numbers, and symbols; avoid birthdays or names.

-Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on email, social media, and banking apps.

-Keep devices updated — regularly install security patches for your phone, laptop, and apps.

-Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks like banking or exam portals; use mobile data or a trusted VPN.

-Think before you click — don’t open suspicious links or attachments in emails, WhatsApp, or Telegram groups.

-Limit personal info online — avoid posting your phone number, address, or college ID openly.

-Review privacy settings — restrict who can see your posts, stories, and tagged photos.

FINANCIAL & UPI SAFETY

-Verify UPI requests carefully — fraudsters may send fake “payment requests.” Always confirm the sender before approving.

-Never share UPI PINs or OTPs — banks and payment apps will never ask for them.

-Check merchant names before paying — scammers often use look‑alike names to trick students during online shopping.
